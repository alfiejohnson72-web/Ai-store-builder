Questions

Good to know

Are your cars HPI checked?

Yes. Each of our MINIs has been fully HPI checked.

Is there a warranty?

Every car comes with our unique 3-month "no quibble" warranty, and our aftersales team will be on hand to answer questions in the weeks and months after you drive away.

Can you arrange finance?

We can arrange same-day finance and drive-away insurance. Finance is subject to status. Representative APR 12.5%.

Can I view a car without an appointment?

Viewings are by appointment only, so we're ready for you. Use the Book button or call 01628 778889.

What if you don't have the MINI I want?

Register your details and our team will do their best to find your ideal MINI through our vehicle sourcing service.

Do you service and MOT MINIs?

Yes, we have a qualified team of technicians and an on-site MOT station.
